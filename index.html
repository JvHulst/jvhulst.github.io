Jilles van Hulst

Senior Mathware Engineer at Sioux Technologies, Eindhoven

I am a Senior Mathware Engineer at Sioux Technologies in Eindhoven. I did my PhD in the Control Systems Technology group at Eindhoven University of Technology, on learning-based calibration, estimation and stage control for electron microscopy, supervised by Maurice Heemels and Duarte Antunes and in collaboration with Thermo Fisher Scientific.

focus a microscope

This is a simulated electron microscope that suffers from aberrations. Turn the knobs until the sample looks sharp, then let the algorithm from my paper try from the same start. The method in the paper starts by summarizing each Ronchigram into two numbers with a variational autoencoder, and expectation maximization works out the aberrations from a series of Ronchigrams taken at different settings. The calibration score is the size of the electron probe in nm, which weighs each aberration by its effect on the image. Try to beat the algorithm!
